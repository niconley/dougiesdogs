About Dougie

Hi, I'm Dougie! Hot dogs aren't just food to me — they're a passion. I've spent years perfecting the art of the perfectly loaded dog, from classic builds to bold new creations that'll keep you coming back for more.

Every single dog is made fresh to order with quality ingredients and a whole lot of heart. Whether you catch us at a local event or book us for your private party, you're guaranteed a great time and an even better meal.
